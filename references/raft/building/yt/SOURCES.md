# Raft building reference frames (YouTube)

Reference stills of the real game Raft (Redbeet Interactive) for internal study of the hammer building system. Not game assets. Never copy anything from this folder into `dist/`.

Frames were pulled from 480p video at a fixed interval, then picked by hand from contact sheets. File names are `<videoid>_<mmss>.jpg`; timestamps are approximate (within a few seconds).

What to look for across the set:

- Build menu: hold RMB with the hammer for a radial-ish panel of categories (foundation, floor, pillar, wall, roof, stairs, repair, replace), each with cost in planks/thatch/nails shown as `have/need`.
- Ghost preview: translucent green when placement is valid, red when blocked. Cost icons float under the crosshair and the on-screen hints read "RMB Hold for more parts", "R Rotate", "MMB Block pick".
- Snapping: foundations snap edge to edge on the grid; pillars, beams, walls and floors snap to foundation edges and pillar tops.
- Decoration (placeables) are held in the empty hand: the hand points at the spot, a ghost follows the surface freely, R rotates in steps and holding R rotates smoothly.
