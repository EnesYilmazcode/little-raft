# Viewmodel reference selection (Raft)

This selection was made from stills already on disk because the network was blocked (YouTube, Steam, raft.wiki.gg and Wikimedia were unreachable). No new images were downloaded, copied or duplicated; every path points at an existing file under `references/raft/`.
For internal study only. Raft is by Redbeet Interactive. Never copy any of this into `dist/`.

Topic: first-person held items and hands. That covers the hook, hammer, spear, fishing rod, cups/bottles and placeables held in hand (Raft has no separate building-plan item: the hammer opens the build menu, and decorations are held in an empty pointing hand with a ghost preview), plus screen position, size, cropping and arm visibility. Ranked best first.

Common pattern: the tool sits in the lower-right third and is cropped by the bottom or right edge. Usually only the right hand and wrist show. Both arms show only for the bow, zipline, swimming, the notebook and the piano. Cups, bottles and food sit lower and smaller than tools. The viewmodel stays drawn while the inventory or build menu is open.
