# Raft wiki item stills

Reference images of the real game Raft (Redbeet Interactive) for internal study only.
Do not ship any of these in dist/ or in game assets. Copyright belongs to the original creators.

Source: the Raft community wiki on Fandom (raft.fandom.com). raft.wiki.gg was tried first
but every page there returned HTTP 401 on 2026-11-20, so the Fandom wiki was used instead.
Images were pulled through the MediaWiki imageinfo API, transparent PNGs flattened onto white,
and saved as JPG (quality 85, at most 1280 px wide).

Notes: Raft has no watering can. Crops are watered by hand with a cup (Empty Cup) of fresh
water, or automatically by a Sprinkler, so both are included. Seeds have no page of their own
(Seeds is a disambiguation), so seed icons come from the crop plot pages.
