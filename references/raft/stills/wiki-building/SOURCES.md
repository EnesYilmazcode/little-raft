# Raft reference stills: building (wiki)

Internal study only. Never ship these in dist/. Images are from the community Raft wiki and are (c) Redbeet Interactive.

Source note: raft.wiki.gg returns HTTP 401 to every non-browser client (curl, WebFetch), and no browser was connected,
so these came from the original community wiki at raft.fandom.com (the same wiki wiki.gg forked from), via its MediaWiki API.
Transparent PNG renders were flattened onto mid gray (128,128,128) and everything was re-encoded to JPG at most 1280 px wide.

Kinds: `screenshot_*` is an in-game screenshot (often a lineup of variants or placement examples);
`piece-render_*` is the wiki infobox render of a single building piece.
