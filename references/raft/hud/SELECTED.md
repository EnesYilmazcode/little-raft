# Raft reference: HUD (selected stills)

Internal study only. These are images of the real game Raft (Redbeet Interactive); never copy them into `dist/`.

The network was blocked during curation (YouTube, Steam, raft.wiki.gg and Wikimedia were all unreachable), so this selection was made only from stills already on disk under `references/raft/`. No images were copied or duplicated; the paths below point at the existing files. Paths are relative to the repo root. Source URLs come from each folder's `SOURCES.md` or `rows.json`. `inventory-crafting/yt` and `farming/yt` have no provenance file, so their URLs are rebuilt from the video id in the filename (`<videoid>_<mmss>.jpg`). Files in `stills/press/steam-store-NN.jpg` duplicate `stills/steam/store_NN.jpg`, so only the `stills/steam` copy is listed.

Ranked best first. 1.0-era means the UI of the full release (June 2022), going by the content visible in the shot.

## Coverage gaps

- No clear frame of the health meter flashing or of a starving/dehydrated warning state, and no death or respawn screen.
- No HUD shot on console or with a controller (button glyphs are always keyboard and mouse).
- The pre-release v1.01 icon HUD is a single wiki image. Early-access 2018 bar HUDs come only from press posts, often with the HUD hidden.
- The oxygen dial appears only in community shots, never in a clean official one.
- Notification toasts appear only in the corner of a frame; no frame shows a long stack or the fade-out animation.
