# Visual QA: Scarecrow, Sky Rockets, Ziplines (round zfs)

Build: dist/ at `1499402` (Zipline review). The shots are headless Chrome with swiftshader at 1280x720 and DPR 1, `?fixedres`.
Baseline: `python shoot.py dist <scratch>/qa-base` ran clean. It had no page errors and only the usual swiftshader `GPU stall` / `KHR_parallel_shader_compile` console noise, and the baseline shots look unchanged.
Feature shots come from `scratchpad/qa_zfs.py` and `qa_extra.py`. They use seeded saves with a 7x5 raft and the `raftDebug.content.{scarecrow,fireworks,zipline}` debug hooks. None of the six sessions logged a page error or a console error/warning.
Copies of the key shots are in `docs/cloud/shots/zfs/`.

## Ranked issues and fixes

1. **The held Sky Rocket, Scarecrow, Glide Post and Low Glide Post show as the generic cloth bundle in the right fist (d1, d2).** They are not in `PLACEABLE_KINDS`/`HOLD`, and the `models.js` factory has no mesh for them, so `buildItemModel` falls back to `buildBundle`. Fix in `dist/held-items.js`:
   - Add `skyrocket`, `scarecrow`, `glidepost` and `glidestep` to the placeable list, so they ride the left hand with the pointing pose like the other decor.
   - Add a `SIZE` entry for each (for example `skyrocket: .2, scarecrow: .22, glidepost: .22, glidestep: .2`).
   - Add a branch that builds them from their own model factories through `fit(...)`: `createRocket(T)` from `fireworks-model.js`, the scarecrow model from `scarecrow-model.js`, and the post models from `zipline-model.js`. `createHandle` already shows the pattern.
   - Candle, beehive and flowerbox probably have the same gap. Check those too.
2. **The fireworks bursts are small and washed out (c-1, c-4).** Three different colour indices all render as similar pale pink/white, and each burst is about 100 px at 12 m. Fix in `dist/fireworks-model.js`, in the shader around lines 93-136:
   - Raise the saturation of the 6-colour palette, or keep the core colour instead of mixing toward white.
   - Bump the burst radius or the spark `size` by about 1.3-1.5x.
   - Lift `uMaxPx` so near bursts are not clamped.
   - Check against the willow, which should still read as gold.
3. **The willow does not read as a willow at its frozen peak (c-3).** Its sparks should visibly droop or trail by t=4. In `fireworks-model.js`, shape 2, give it stronger gravity and longer trail `lag` so a falling streak forms within about 0.6 s of the burst. Also make the spec QA freeze point (`t=4.0`) show the droop.
4. **The gull clips into the palm trunk while pecking (a1).** The spec (§46) asks that wingtips not clip the pot. The body also overlaps the trunk. Fix in `dist/scarecrow.js` `rimSpot()` / `RIM_OFFSET` (currently .3 m): push it out to about .38-.4 m so the gull sits on the rim lip. Also face the gull outward or tangent, toward the trunk only with the head, in the `wildlife.js` palm perch.
5. **The Sky Rocket model is very thin on the deck (d1, d2).** A tube of r .035 is a hairline at a few metres and nearly invisible at night, which goes against MODEL-STYLE's "chunky beats realistic". Fix in `dist/fireworks-model.js` `rocketParts`:
   - Thicken `TUBE` to about .05 and scale the cone and stick to match, or scale the whole group by about 1.3.
   - Consider a faint warm emissive on the cream band, or a tiny fuse spark, so it can be found after dark, which is when players use it.
6. **The zipline ride hand pose reads as "holding" rather than "hanging" (f1).** The handle is at chest height and its wheel stem points forward, not up at the rope. Fix in `dist/hands.js` / `held-items.js` (the ride raise added in 1499402): while `zipline.pose().riding`, raise the right hand higher toward the top of the screen and rotate the handle so the wheel stem points up (+Y toward the line). A short rope-coloured segment from the wheel to the top edge of the screen would sell the connection.
7. **At the end of the ride the rider's face meets the low post (f3/probe).** The post body fills the screen at about 1.1 m. Fix in `dist/zipline.js` `ride()`/dismount: end the ride (the hop) about 0.6-0.8 m before the wheel, or offset the rider's hang point sideways by about 0.3 m from the line, so the mast passes beside the camera and not through the view.
8. **The gull on the scarecrow hat is oversized compared with the scarecrow (b2), and the arms are thin.** Fix in `dist/scarecrow-model.js`: scale the scarecrow up about 1.15x, raising `HAT_TOP` in `scarecrow.js` and `wildlife` `PERCH_TOP` to match, and widen the hat brim. Thicken the arm sticks to at least .04 m. Or scale only the perched gull down to about .85 on the `scarecrow` perch kind.
9. **The Glide Post and Low Glide Post hotbar icons are near-identical (d3).** Fix in `dist/item-svg.js`: give the low post a visibly shorter, stubbier mast plus a small down-arrow or step mark, or a different wheel colour.
10. Low priority, older: the material icons swap from SVG to 3D render at runtime, so the rope and nail look changes. This happens in `item-icons.js` / `survival-ui.js` `iconFor`, and the new SVG-only items sit in the same bag as 3D-rendered ones. Not caused by this round.

## After fixes

The fixes are in the working tree and not committed. `npm test` passes all 680 tests. I re-ran the shots with `qa_zfs.py` (fireworks, rocket, protect, inventory, scarecrow) and `qa_extra.py`, plus three new probes in the scratchpad: `qa_fix_probe.py` (a side view of the rim gull), `qa_fix_ride.py` (the last riding frame on a slow uphill line, then the frame after the hop) and `qa_fix_held.py` (every new held item).
