# HUD spec (hotbar, stat bars, crosshair, prompts, hold ring, pickup feed, notices, oxygen)

Scope: how Raft (Redbeet Interactive) lays out its in-play HUD, studied from reference stills only, and what Little Raft should change. This is an internal study. Nothing here is an asset to copy. The change list recommends layout, readability and feel, not Raft's skin (its wood-plank frame, painted icons, fonts and key-cap art stay Raft's).

Little Raft has no hunger, thirst, health or damage by design (README.md line 3, and the help text "Nothing can hurt you"). Stat-bar findings are recorded only as layout space and optional ideas, never as a request to add survival meters.

Method: each image was opened and inspected. Boxes were measured with Pillow and numpy by scanning pixel rows and columns for the frame edges (scripts and 2-3x crops are in the session scratchpad under `hud/`). Colours are the median of a 5x5 patch (3x3 or 1 px for thin features), given with image and pixel coordinates. Positions are % of the frame: x as % of width (W), y as % of height (H), measured from the top-left. All Steam stills are 1280x720 (store shots are 1920x1080 originals scaled down); yt frames are 960x540.

## 2. Observations / spec

### Layout overview
- **C1. Four corners, one centre.** Survival bars bottom-left, hotbar bottom-centre, pickup feed bottom-right, research card top-right, oxygen gauge top-centre (only underwater), crosshair dead centre. The top-left and most of the top edge stay empty during normal play. Evidence: every primary image; `community_084` (top-right card), `community_090` (top-centre gauge).
- **C2. The HUD scales with resolution, not in fixed pixels.** Store shots (1920x1080 originals) and community shots (1280x720) give the same hotbar width in % (C3), so the UI is proportional to the screen. Evidence: `store_04`, `store_02` vs `community_069`, `community_147`.
- **C3. The HUD remains visible under menus.** With crafting and inventory open, the hotbar and bars stay drawn and the crosshair becomes a cursor. Evidence: `store_04`, `68lj91mBrDI_0149`.

### Crosshair
- **C17. A thin ring with a centre dot, exactly at 50/50.** Ring diameter 31-33 px = 4.3-4.6% H (median 4.5% H, n=4: `_069` (-16,+15), `_147` (-17,+16), `_090`, `_063`). Ring stroke about 1 px. Centre dot about 4-5 px (0.6% H). Colour: white at partial opacity (on the dark sea it blends to `#5c7c93`, `_069` 655,359; the dot centre is `#cde1ea`).
- **C18. Legibility gap: the ring disappears on bright sky.** In `community_122` a threshold scan finds no ring pixels (bg luminance 619/765). Raft has no dark outline. Little Raft should do better here.

### Interaction prompts
- **C19. Key-cap + verb, stacked: the cap above, the text below, no background box.** Key cap 22 px tall (3.0% H), about 20 px wide for one letter, 33 px for "RMB" (2.6% W), cream face `#e7d4b2` (`_069`), `#e6d2b2` (`_115`), `#e7d3b3` (`_063`). The text is cream-white with a soft shadow and 16-20 px cap height.
- **C20. Two prompt anchors.** (a) World interactions (E: pick up, lay down, use) sit **above** the crosshair: the cap centre is at 39.9% H, the text at about 43.6% H, so the stack ends about 6% H above centre (`community_063`; also `devblog-38-09`, 480 px). (b) Held-item actions (RMB choose bait, LMB drink, X remove) sit **just above the hotbar**: the cap is at y 578-598 (80.3-83.1% H), the text at about 85-86% H (`_069`, `_084`, `_115`, n=3). Several actions form one centred row. In `_115` the cap centres are 66 px apart (5.2% W).

### Hold-to-pick-up ring
- **C21. A thick arc around the crosshair fills clockwise from 12 o'clock.** Outer radius 25 px, inner 18 px: outer Ø 50 px = 6.9% H, band about 7-8 px = 1.0% H. Cream `#f0e0c6`-`#f9e3ce` (`_063` 658-664,359). In `_063` it is about 45-50% full (it covers 12 o'clock at y 334-341, and 6 o'clock at y 377-384 is still empty). ### Pickup feed ("+1" notices)
- **C22. One row per item, stacked, bottom-right, flush with the right edge.** Panel x 1113-1280 = 87.0-100% W (13.0% W). Row height 33 px (4.6% H). ## 3. Little Raft today

Effective values after the cascade style.css -> graphics.css -> injected CSS (game.js `HUD_CSS`, survival-ui.js `CSS`):

- **Hotbar.** 8 slots (`inventory-state.js:6` `HOTBAR_SIZE = 8`; `game.js:37` `HOTBAR = 8`; keys 1-8 and the wheel, `game.js:899,906`). The slots are fixed **58x58 px** (`survival-ui.js:122`) with gap 4, padding 5 and border 2 (`survival-ui.js:117-119`), so the bar is about 506x72 px. That is 39.5% W at 1280x720 but 26% W at 1920x1080; the height is 10% H at 720p and 6.7% at 1080p. It does not scale with the screen (C2). The bar floats 20 px above the bottom (`graphics.css:111` `.bottom{bottom:20px}`), while Raft is flush (C5). Below 760 px wide the slots drop to 42 px (`survival-ui.js:136`).
- **Slot contents.** A slot number at top-left (`.sv-num`, `survival-ui.js:127`), the count at bottom-right (`:130`), and the icon inset 9 px (`:128`). The selected slot gets a lighter gradient plus a 2 px inset `#ffe3a6` outline (`survival-ui.js:135`). It is clear, but it is not enlarged and it is drawn inside the slot (C8 draws it outside and larger).
- **Durability.** A 3 px strip, left/right 6 px, bottom 3 px, on a `#0009` track (`survival-ui.js:131`). The colour bands are >50% `#8fd36a`, >25% `#ffbd66`, otherwise `#e06a4a` (`survival-ui.js:254`). The behaviour matches C10, but the strip is 5% of the slot height vs about 11% in Raft, so it is hard to read at a glance. The separate "HOOK CONDITION" panel is hidden during play (`game.js:46` hides `#durability`).
- **Crosshair.** The final rule is `graphics.css:384`: a 23 px ring with a 1 px `#fff9` border, `background:none!important`, and a centre dot `::after` 2x2 px (`graphics.css:219-222`). Ring = 3.2% H at 720p and 2.1% at 1080p (Raft 4.5%). The dot is half Raft's size. `style.css:9` also draws a `:before` 2 px ring at `inset:0`, which `graphics.css` does not cancel, so there may be two concentric rings (see Q1). A drop shadow `0 1px 1px #0005` gives little contrast on bright sky (C18).
- **Prompts and notices share one line.** `#contextPrompt` sits **below** the crosshair at `top:calc(50% + 26px)`, a single line with an inline `<kbd>` on a `rgba(8,22,36,.55)` pill (`game.js:48-51`). `updateContext` (`game.js:401-412`) shows one message by priority: guest waiting > **notice** > "E Pick up X" > floor cost > "Hold click to reel". A loot notice ("+ 2 Wood · 1 Plastic", `game.js:562`, held 1.8 s by `notice()`, `game.js:394`) therefore **hides the pickup prompt** for the next item. Crafts, repairs, warnings and loot all compete for this one slot (`effect()`, `game.js:526-530`). Net catches are silent (`if (!a.net)`, `game.js:556,562`).
- **Charge meter.** A horizontal 158x23 px "THROW POWER" bar 44 px below the crosshair (`graphics.css:367`), driven by `updateCharge` (`game.js:414-419`, `CHARGE_MS = 900`).
- **Hold actions.** Pickup is an instant E press (`game.js:612-621`). Climbing aboard is a timed clamber with `climbProgress` exposed (`player-physics.js:67`) but not shown in the HUD.
- **Hidden in play.** Header, goal card, controls bar, toast and aimHint are all `display:none` while playing (`game.js:46-47`, `graphics.css:382`). Only the supplies card stays, top-left (`graphics.css:383` `.playing .inventory{top:14px;left:14px}`), which Raft keeps empty (C1). The inventory key (E when not aiming at an item, `game.js:621`) is not taught anywhere on the in-play HUD.
- **No equivalents:** no stat bars (by design), no oxygen (the player only swims at the surface, `player-physics.js:20-21`), and no research notices.

## 4. Ranked change list

1. **#1 Scale the hotbar with the viewport and dock it to the bottom.** (`dist/survival-ui.js:117-122,136`: slot size `clamp(40px, 6.2vh, 72px)`, gap about 0.5vw, slot pitch about 3.8% W, so 8 slots are about 31% W at 16:9; `dist/graphics.css:111`: `.bottom` bottom about 0.6vh instead of 20px.) Keeps proportions at 720p and 1080p (C2, C5-C7). Impact H, effort S.
2. **#2 Split notices from prompts: add a stacked pickup feed at the bottom-right.** (`dist/game.js`: new `#pickupFeed` in `HUD_CSS`; in `effect()` route `kind:'loot'` there instead of `notice()`; emit a quiet aggregated row for net catches too (`game.js:556-562`).) One row per item kind with "+N", icon and name, row about 4.5% H, panel about 13% W, right edge flush, max 4 rows, newest at the bottom, merging same-kind gains within 1 s, fading after about 3 s. Use Little Raft's own panel colours (teal/cream), not Raft's. This stops loot text from hiding the next "E Pick up" prompt (C22). Impact H, effort M.
3. **#3 Two prompt anchors with stacked key + verb.** (`dist/game.js:48-51,401-412`.) World interactions ("E Pick up Wood") go **above** the crosshair, ending about 6% H above centre (C20a). Held-item hints (hook: "Click hold Throw", "R Repair"; rod: "Click Cast"; hammer: "Click Build", plus the floor-cost line) go in a centred row about 82-86% H, just above the hotbar (C20b). Draw the key as a small light cap (about 3% H tall) above a short verb, with a soft shadow instead of a pill. Keep notices out of these slots (#2, #9). Impact H, effort M.
4. **#4 Bigger, legible crosshair, and remove the possible double ring.** (`dist/graphics.css:384` and `:219-222`; `dist/style.css:7-9`.) Ring about 4vh (`clamp(24px,4vh,40px)`), 1.5 px stroke at 75% opacity, 4 px dot, and a 1 px dark outline (`0 0 0 1px #0006`) so it survives bright sky (C17, C18). Cancel the `style.css` `:before` ring or make it the only ring (Q1). Impact M, effort S.
5. **#5 One radial progress ring around the crosshair for every hold/charge.** (`dist/graphics.css:367` replace the `#chargeMeter` bar; `dist/game.js:414-419` drive a conic-gradient or SVG arc.) Outer about 7% H, band about 1% H, starting at 12 o'clock and filling clockwise (C21). Use it for hook throw power (`chargeValue()`), the climb-aboard clamber (`player-physics.js:67` `climbProgress`) and any future hold action. Keeps the eye on the crosshair instead of a bar below it. Impact M-H, effort M.
6. **#6 Thicker durability strip.** (`dist/survival-ui.js:131`.) Height about 10% of the slot (5-6 px at 58 px), about 85% of slot width, 2 px from the bottom, dark track with a 1 px outline. Keep the 3-band colours (`:254`), but use a yellow middle band (closer to C10) so "half worn" differs from "almost broken". Impact M, effort S.
7. **#7 Make the selected slot pop outward.** (`dist/survival-ui.js:135`.) Add `transform:scale(1.08)` (Raft's selected slot is +10%, C8) and a 2 px light outline outside the slot instead of inset, keeping the current lighter fill. Add `transition:transform .08s` and respect the existing reduced-motion rule (`:186`). Impact M, effort S.
8. **#8 Teach the bag key with a small end tab on the hotbar.** (`dist/survival-ui.js:344-360` `ensureTools`: append a non-slot tab "E Bag" hanging off the left end, about 2.5% W x 4% H, `pointer-events:auto` and opening the inventory.) The controls bar is hidden in play (`graphics.css:382`), so nothing teaches the key now (C12). Impact M, effort S.
9. **#9 Top-right card for crafted / new-recipe moments.** (`dist/game.js:546` `kind:'craft'`, and the "build" events at `:579`/`:592`.) Card about 17% W x 9% H, 2.4% from top and right, icon + "Crafted Collection Net" + a second line "by your brother" in co-op (C24's credit line suits Little Raft's two-player play). Show for 3-4 s and stack at most 2. Impact M, effort S-M.
10. **#10 Move the top-left supplies card out of the play view, or shrink it.** (`dist/graphics.css:383` `.playing .inventory`.) The bag and hotbar now hold supplies, and Raft keeps the top-left empty (C1). Either hide it while playing or reduce it to a small count chip near the feed. Impact L-M, effort S.
11. **#11 Reserve the bottom-left block; optional hook-condition gauge there.** No hunger, thirst or health (README). If anything goes in the Raft stat-bar zone (x 1-15% W, y 84-97% H, C13), make it a single slim "hook condition" bar shown only below 25% or while repairing (`game.js:536-537` already emit the warnings). Otherwise leave it empty for a calmer screen. Impact L, effort S.
12. **#12 Oxygen: note only.** If diving is ever added, put a compact breath gauge top-centre, flush with the top edge, about 11% W x 11% H, draining from one end and shown only underwater (C25), drawn in Little Raft's own style. No change now. Impact L, effort -.

## 5. Open questions / UNSURE

- **Q1.** Is the crosshair a double ring? `style.css:9` (`#crosshair:before`, 2 px, inset 0) is not overridden by `graphics.css:384`. It needs a quick render to confirm. Not verified, because I did not run the game.
- **Q2.** Raft's hold-to-pick-up duration and the toast lifetime and fade time cannot be measured from stills. The numbers above are estimates (from memory, unverified).
- **Q3.** The order of the pickup feed (newest at top or bottom) is not decidable from `community_147`. Pick newest-at-bottom for Little Raft (closest to the hotbar, where the eye returns).
- **Q4.** `store_16`/`store_22` show a larger hotbar (41.9% W, no end tabs), probably an older build or a different UI-scale setting. The 38.75% median comes from the 1.0-era shots.
- **Q5.** With 8 slots, matching Raft's slot pitch gives about 31% W, not 39%. That is intentional: keep the slot size (about 6% H), not the total width.
- **Q6.** The threshold tick on Raft's bars (C14) is read from a 3x crop only. Its exact % is not measured.
