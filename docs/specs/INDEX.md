# Feature specs

Implementable specs for builders. One line per spec.

- [notes-achievements.md](notes-achievements.md): message bottles (12 notes), 15 achievements, the L-key raft logbook (dist/logbook.js, logbook-ui.js, bottle.js)
- [receiver-navigation.md](receiver-navigation.md): receiver + up to 3 antennas detect a drifting signal cache; HUD bearing pill, edge marker, cache loot (dist/signal.js, signal-models.js)
- [purifier-watering.md](purifier-watering.md): cups (cup/saltcup/freshcup), placeable Water Purifier (15 s), watering and harvesting Palm Planters (90 s, 3 growth stages); world.garden/world.purifiers (dist/watering.js, purifier-model.js)
- [sleep.md](sleep.md): craftable Bed decor; sleep at dusk/night with fade, skip to morning via host-only daynight.skipTo; co-op needs both in bed (dist/sleep.js, sleep-model.js)
- [engine.md](engine.md): Little Motor (E toggle, leaf/wood/plastic fuel, diminishing push along the shared heading, cap 2.0 m/s) + Steering Wheel as an extra hold-R steering target; world.ext.engine, opts.push in stepSailing (dist/engine.js, engine-models.js)
- [paint.md](paint.md): Paint Brush (stack-1 item) + 5 soft paint pots (4 per craft, 1 per piece/tile) and free Driftwood scrape; click paints, right click cycles colour; instanced setColorAt tint for pieces, cached wood materials for deck tiles; world.ext.paint {v,rev,m} (dist/paint.js, brush-model.js)
- [instruments.md](instruments.md): held Tide Drum / Reed Pipes / Bell Bars (tool:false, stack 1); LMB/RT plays, camera pitch picks 1 of 8 D-pentatonic notes; audio.note() on the effects bus; transient co-op {type:'inst'} with falloff; no world state (dist/instruments.js, instrument-models.js)
- [rain-sprinkler.md](rain-sprinkler.md): Rain Barrel (fills in rain, 4 cups, E with empty cup -> freshcup) + Sprinkler (3-unit tank or adjacent barrel, auto-waters dry palms in 8 neighbours); world.collectors/world.sprinklers, daynight rain getter (dist/rain.js, rain-model.js)
- [beehive.md](beehive.md): Beehive + Flower Box + honey/wax; hive fills from flower boxes within 2 tiles, E collects honey and beeswax for a candle and honey glazed fish; flower seed from island bushes and barrels (dist/bees.js, bees-model.js)
- [customise.md](customise.md): "Look" dialog (pause menu + welcome card) for shirt/trousers/skin/hair swatches and 5 hats unlocked by logbook achievements; localStorage little-raft-look-v1, co-op hello look + k:'look' event, remote rebake, hands.setLook (dist/look.js, look-ui.js)
- [maptable.md](maptable.md): Map Table (items.js decor, copper tier) opens a parchment chart: -z up, 4 scale presets, raft + heading, decimated trail, charted islands with seed names and visited/gathered ticks, signal wedge via signalReadout, 8 shared pins, Set course via sail heading; world.ext.maptable {v,rev,seq,isl,pins,trail}, content peek(name) (dist/maptable.js, maptable-ui.js, maptable-model.js)
- [treasure.md](treasure.md): Seashell Finder (never wears) + Little Spade (60 digs, R repair) and 1-2 seeded buried bundles per island from rng(seed ^ K); detector ring HUD + rising detect/chime beeps, E digs (raftPrompts provider), mound + chest pop; islandNow() accessor; world.ext.treasure {v,seed,dug} (dist/treasure.js, treasure-rules.js, treasure-models.js)
- [kiln.md](kiln.md): Seaside Kiln (research-gated: stone+clay+scrap) smelts metal ore/sand/clay into Metal Bar/Glass Pane/Fired Brick with wood fuel; Glass Lantern, Glass Cups, Brick Planters, Line Sinker; world.ext.kiln (dist/kiln.js, kiln-core.js, kiln-model.js)
- [raft-animals.md](raft-animals.md): Puffhen v1: Nest Box (starter) lure, seeded host-clock wander (no positions sent), feed/pet/collect/rename, eggs and feathers, Egg Lantern and Feather Cushion; world.ext.animals (dist/animals.js, animals-core.js, animals-models.js, animals-ui.js)
- [decor-signs.md](decor-signs.md): decoration pack (Deck Mat, Wind Pennant, Fern Post, Shell Chime, Hammock, Clay Pot Trio) + writable Signboard (3×18-char lines, CanvasTexture cache), quarter-turn rotation for all 7; world.ext.decorpack (dist/decor-pack.js, decor-pack-models.js, signs-core.js, signs-ui.js)
- [juicer.md](juicer.md): Juicer + fruit smoothies; bush berries, planter coconuts, instant E-blend (1 fresh cup + 2 fruit -> smoothie cup, host-checked), 3 drinks that return the cup, honeyfish FOOD fix, drink cues and E-over-eat prompt fix (dist/juicer.js, juicer-model.js)
- [companion-gull.md](companion-gull.md): Companion Gull + Gull Perch (woven tray); treats on a 90 s visit schedule (3 points to move in), host-clock rest/loop/follow modes, pet/name/heart burst, finds tray (3, all-or-nothing); world.ext.gull (dist/gull.js, gull-models.js)
- [building-pack.md](building-pack.md): Railing, Hatch (E toggles; o:1 only while open), Ladder (MoveFeel setLadder climb, preStep virtual ground) and optional Gate; appended to PIECE_ORDER, toRec keeps o, _scan/_ramps special cases, host piecetoggle (dist/building-pieces.js, building.js, building-models.js)
- [diving-gear.md](diving-gear.md): carry-to-use dive gear (Puff-Feather Fins swim x1.3, Breath Jug 75 s air, Seaglass Goggles fog 46 m, Tide Scraper gather x0.6), research-gated; shared `diveMods(m)` multiplier hook {swim,oxy,fog,gather} for meals too; HUD gear chips + goggles mask; no new save keys (dist/diving-gear.js, diving-gear-core.js)
- [trader.md](trader.md): Pim's trading barge docks at the bow (first visit ~4 min, then every 10-14 min, 4 min stay); 6 seeded offers filtered by ITEM_INFO, Bright Shell currency, atomic trades, Glass Float rare decor, Shell Wreath plan (one-line setOwnedElsewhere edit); world.ext.trader (dist/trader.js, trader-core.js, trader-model.js, trader-ui.js)
- [cookpot.md](cookpot.md): Cooking Pot (research-gated, same studies as the kiln) cooks 3-ingredient recipes with wood into 2 servings; Driftwood Bowl; 6 `meal_*` meals eaten with E give one timed per-player buff (swim/walk/jump/oxygen/reel/night floor) via MOVE_MUL, HOOK_MUL, FISHING_TUNING and the shared diveMods hook; HUD buff chip; world.ext.cookpot {k,b,r,br} (dist/cookpot.js, cookpot-core.js, cookpot-model.js)
- [gametable.md](gametable.md): Driftwood Game Table (research-gated: wood+rope+stone): draughts "Shells & Pebbles" (forced capture, multi-jump, kings, 40-ply calm-seas draw) + noughts and crosses "Knots & Hoops"; Crewmate or host-run Gull AI (Easy/Clever); E opens the #lrGame board dialog (mouse/touch/arrows+Enter); instanced 3D pieces; gt.* actions with move counter n; no achievement; world.ext.gametable (dist/gametable.js, gametable-core.js, gametable-model.js, gametable-ui.js)
- [paddleboard.md](paddleboard.md): Driftboard (`driftboard`, research-gated wood+plastic+rope): left click launches it at the deck edge; tied/adrift (drifts home)/ridden; kinematic stroke paddling on ocean.getHeight with wave tilt, soft tether 38 m / hard 45 m, hull push-out, beaching via islands; zipline-identical `content.ride/riding` hook + `rideIn` (+strafe/sprint/yaw/interact); world.ext.paddleboard (dist/paddleboard.js, paddleboard-core.js, paddleboard-model.js)
- [buoycourse.md](buoycourse.md): Buoy Ring Course time trials: Course Kit crafts the Start Flag decor (pole E start/stop, board E cycles Loop/Sprint/Zigzag/Figure 8 generated from the raft bbox) + Race Scoreboard; 3-2-1 countdown, local segment-plane ring crossing in order, bell per ring via contentCtx `note` (one-line game.js edit, also `get content()`), timer pill #buoyTimer; top-5 per shape per mode (swim/board via physics.mount==='driftboard'); host validates buoy.finish (gate hash + min time); stops when the raft moves; ≤3 ring draw calls; world.ext.buoycourse (dist/buoycourse.js, buoycourse-core.js, buoycourse-model.js)
