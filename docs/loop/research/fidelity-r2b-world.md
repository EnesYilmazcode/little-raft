## Top 4 gaps (ranked by player impact)

1. **Islands that stay put: sail to them and anchor or drift past.** The island loop (approach, dock, depart) runs on its own clock, so the sail, anchor and heading never matter for the best content. Sketch: in `islands.js`/`islands-layout.js`, spawn the island 120-160 m ahead along the flow direction (`sailing-world.js` `flowDir`, about 40 m to the side), and move it each frame by the same seabed offset (`out.dgx/dgz`) that moves scenery. That is about 0.3 m/s at drift and about 1.3 m/s under sail, so a passing island is reachable for about 60-120 s unless anchored. Remove the `docked`/`DOCK` phase from `pathAt`. Despawn the island once it is more than 150 m behind (`ISLAND_WRAP` 150), then wait `CALM_MIN/MAX` 150-240 s before the next. Keep the existing grounding blockers (`sailing-world.js:94`) so the raft beaches instead of passing through. Add a HUD or notice cue ("Land ahead") when the island spawns.

2. **Axe and real palm chopping.** Raft gates wood from land behind a tool. Sketch: add `axe` to `items.js` (tool, durability about 40, recipe 6 wood + 3 stone + 2 rope, the Stone Axe cost from a guide, `unlock: research('stone','rope')`) plus a held model in `held-items.js`. In `islands-layout.js`, give a palm `NODE_MAX` 5 hits that need `toolRole === 'axe'` (refuse with "Needs an axe" otherwise). Each chop gives 1 wood, with a 17.6% coconut and 11.8% palm seed roll from `hash3`. The felling chop adds +1 leaf twice, for 5 planks + 2 leaves in total. Bushes stay bare-hand. Wear 1 use per chop (`wearPerUse`).

3. **Debris tied to raft motion, so anchoring has a cost.** Today an anchored raft still gets a 0.3 m/s stream of debris. Sketch: in `game.js` `updateItems` (`:1347`), keep the despawn but gate `makeItem` respawns with a budget: `budget += dt * k * |v_raft over water|` with k about 0.6 items per metre, filling at most 30 items. When `state.anchored`, set the water flow (`sailing-core.js:152` `out.dwx/dwz`) to 0 so debris stops near the raft instead of streaming past. Spawn upstream along the travel direction (already `placeUpstream`). Result: drifting at 0.3 m/s gives about 1 new item every 5-6 s, sailing gives about 1 every 1.3 s, anchored gives none.

4. **Paddle item (wire up the dead `paddle` op).** Without it a raft with no sail cannot steer toward an island (needed by #1). Sketch: add a `paddle` tool to `items.js` (recipe 4 wood + 2 rope (U), durability about 60). In `game.js`, a LMB stroke while it is held and the player stands on an edge tile sends `{kind:'sail', op:'paddle', angle:yaw}` to `sailing-core.js:190`. Use the existing `PADDLE_IMPULSE` 0.25 with `PADDLE_COOLDOWN` 0.8 s, and raise `PADDLE_CAP` from 0.9 to about 1.1 to match Raft's +0.8 m/s over drift. Add a stroke animation in `hands.js` and a splash from `waterfx.js`.

Minor: add rope (about 7%, 1-2) to the barrel basic pool. Research table cost 6+2 → 14 wood + 2 scrap. Split the crafting tabs into Tools, Equipment, Navigation, Food/Water, Building, Other. Fix the `items.js:44` HOTBAR_SIZE mismatch.
