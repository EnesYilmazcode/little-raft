# Raft fidelity research, round 1: shark, spear, foundation repair

The wiki hosts were DNS-blocked, so these numbers come from search summaries of raft.fandom.com (Shark, Wooden/Metal Spear), namu.wiki, gamerant and progameguides. Lines marked [G] are our own tuning.

## Shark
- HP 150. It always circles the raft, about 10 m radius at about 3.5 m/s, with the fin breaking the surface [G].
- It attacks the raft every 300 s on Normal. The first attack comes after about 300 s [G], so a new player has time to craft a spear.
- Target: a random edge foundation without armor. If every edge tile is armored, it skips the attack.
- It bites until the tile breaks or it is driven off. A foundation has 100 HP and each bite deals 20, one bite every 2 s, so about 10 s per tile [G].
- 15 damage within one attack repels it: 3 wooden-spear hits. It then goes back to circling and the timer restarts.
- A destroyed foundation takes everything built on it.
- A player in the water becomes the target: 30 damage per bite, one lunge every 3.5 s [G].
- Damage taken: wooden spear 5, metal spear 10. The hook does nothing.
- When it dies the body floats; hold E to harvest 4 raw shark meat and 1 shark head. It respawns 180 s after being looted.
- Cues: dorsal fin, swim sound, a sting when an attack starts, and a raft shake and creak on each bite [G].

## Spear
- Wooden spear: 8 planks + 3 rope, 5 damage, 20 uses (1 per hit that lands).
- Metal spear: 6 planks + 2 rope + 2 metal ingots + 1 bolt, 10 damage, 40 uses.
- LMB thrusts. Cooldown 0.7 s, reach 2.8 m [G].

## Repair and armor
- With the hammer, LMB on a damaged foundation costs 1 plank and restores 50% HP. It works while the shark is biting.
- Foundation armor costs 1 metal ingot + 2 nails, and the shark ignores an armored tile.
